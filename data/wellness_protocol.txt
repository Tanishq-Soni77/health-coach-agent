WELLNESS PROTOCOL — 30-DAY FOUNDATIONAL HEALTH PROGRAM
Version 1.0

=== OVERVIEW ===
This protocol is a 30-day foundational program focused on sleep, movement, nutrition, and stress management. It is designed for general wellness and is NOT a substitute for medical advice.

=== WEEK 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1–7) ===

Day 1: Orientation
- Goal: Understand your baseline.
- Task: Log your current sleep time, energy level (1–10), and water intake.
- Do: Drink at least 8 glasses of water today.
- Don't: Make any major diet changes on Day 1. Observe first.

Day 2: Sleep Audit
- Goal: Improve sleep hygiene.
- Do: Set a consistent bedtime (ideally 10–11 PM).
- Do: Avoid screens 30 minutes before bed.
- Don't: Use caffeine after 2 PM.
- Tip: Ideal sleep duration is 7–9 hours for adults.

Day 3: Movement Introduction
- Goal: Begin light daily movement.
- Do: Walk for 20–30 minutes at a comfortable pace.
- Don't: Push yourself to intense exercise yet.
- Note: Morning walks improve cortisol regulation.

Day 4: Nutrition Basics
- Do: Add one serving of vegetables to each meal.
- Do: Eat breakfast within 1 hour of waking.
- Don't: Skip meals. Skipping meals increases cortisol.
- Avoid: Processed sugar in the morning.

Day 5: Hydration & Energy
- Do: Start morning with 500ml of water before coffee or tea.
- Do: Track your energy levels before and after meals.
- Don't: Drink more than 2 cups of coffee per day.

Day 6: Stress Awareness
- Do: Practice 5 minutes of deep breathing or meditation.
- Do: Write down 3 things you are grateful for.
- Don't: Check email or social media within the first 30 minutes of waking.

Day 7: Week 1 Review
- Reflect on: Sleep quality, energy levels, water intake.
- Celebrate small wins.
- Don't: Be harsh on yourself for missed days. Progress > perfection.

=== WEEK 2: BUILD (Days 8–14) ===

Day 8–10: Increase Movement
- Do: Increase walk duration to 40 minutes OR add light strength training (2x per week).
- Don't: Skip rest days. Recovery is part of the protocol.

Day 11–14: Meal Structure
- Do: Follow a loose meal structure — breakfast, lunch, dinner with no snacking after 8 PM.
- Do: Include protein in every meal.
- Avoid: Eating large meals within 2 hours of bedtime.

=== WEEK 3: DEEPEN (Days 15–21) ===
- Focus: Habit consistency.
- Do: Maintain a sleep log.
- Do: Add 10 minutes of intentional stretching or yoga.
- Don't: Add new habits — reinforce existing ones.

=== WEEK 4: INTEGRATE (Days 22–30) ===
- Focus: Make habits automatic.
- Do: Plan next week's meals on Sunday.
- Do: Review progress and set 3 goals for the next 30 days.
- Don't: Abandon the protocol just because it feels easy — that means it's working.

=== DOS AND DON'TS (GENERAL) ===

DO:
- Drink 2–3 litres of water daily.
- Sleep 7–9 hours per night.
- Move your body for at least 20 minutes daily.
- Eat whole foods as much as possible.
- Practice stress-reduction techniques daily (breathing, journaling, gratitude).
- Be consistent over being perfect.

DON'T:
- Use this protocol as a substitute for medical treatment.
- Crash diet or make extreme changes overnight.
- Use caffeine after 2 PM.
- Exercise intensely every day without rest.
- Skip sleep to compensate for a busy schedule.
- Ignore persistent symptoms — consult a doctor.

=== TRACKING RULES ===
- Log daily: energy (1–10), sleep hours, water glasses, movement (yes/no).
- Weekly: Review your 7-day average for each metric.
- If energy consistently below 4/10 for 3+ days: rest and reassess.
- If sleep < 6 hours for 3+ nights in a row: prioritise sleep above all other habits.

=== SUPPLEMENTS (OPTIONAL, NOT REQUIRED) ===
- Vitamin D3: 1000–2000 IU daily (especially if limited sun exposure).
- Magnesium glycinate: 200–400mg before bed (supports sleep).
- Omega-3: 1–2g daily (supports inflammation and mood).
Note: Always consult a doctor before starting supplements.

=== EMERGENCY GUIDANCE ===
If you experience chest pain, severe dizziness, or persistent unusual symptoms during this protocol, stop and consult a healthcare professional immediately. This protocol is not for acute medical conditions.
